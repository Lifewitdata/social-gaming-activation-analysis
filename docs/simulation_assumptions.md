# Simulation assumptions and limitations

## Provenance
All records are generated by `src/generate_data.py` using NumPy's random generator with seed 2026. IDs are artificial and no real users or proprietary product records are used. The scenario is inspired by consumer social gaming, not an assertion about any company's actual funnel.

## Population and observation
10,000 registration timestamps are uniform across 90 days starting January 1, 2026 UTC. Observation stops at April 1, 2026 UTC. Sessions, events and active queues are truncated at that boundary. This creates genuine follow-up censoring for activation and retention. Daily seasonality and growth are not simulated.

## Planted mechanisms
- Channel proportions: organic 32%, referral 18%, paid social 35%, influencer 15%.
- Paid social has a larger low-tier-device share (40% vs 25% for other channels) and lower latent intent on average.
- Latent intent is a beta-distributed variable shifted by channel and clipped to [0.05,0.98]. It influences onboarding, queue patience, game completion and later session frequency. It is deliberately **not exported** to emulate an unmeasured confounder.
- Version 1.1 appears from February 15: approximately 76% of registrations after that point use it. Earlier users remain on 1.0; upgrades are not modelled.
- A candidate technical failure is sampled with probability 25% for version 1.1/low-tier vs 2.5% elsewhere, plus 7 percentage points on poor mobile networks. Actual failure rates are lower because match success or abandonment can happen first.
- Match wait, patience and technical failure time compete. Match wait is exponential with a longer mean on poor networks; patience depends on latent intent. Observed queue time is the minimum of these times.
- Failed/abandoned attempts can be followed by up to two retries in a session. Users can also return later within 24 hours.
- Game-start success is lower for low-tier version 1.1 users. Game completion depends on latent intent and network quality.
- Later sessions are independently sampled for days 1–30 using intent and a decay term. **Activation does not cause retention in this generator.** An observed association can arise from shared causes.
- Only one sampled user's participation per game is generated. These data support user funnel analytics, not room occupancy, player-network effects, matchmaking supply, or multiplayer balance analysis.

## Data-quality exercise
The raw events contain 100 copied rows, 30 extra events dated before registration, and 20 extra events with unknown users. They are shuffled. Validation quarantines bad rows and never overwrites raw input. A rejected row may violate multiple checks; reason counts therefore are not additive.

## Appropriate interpretation
Statistical intervals quantify uncertainty under the simulation, not uncertainty about Tamasha's business. Results demonstrate pipeline correctness and reasoning. Do not describe planted patterns as newly discovered real-world product problems. Expected benefits in the recommendation are explicit scenarios and have not been achieved.

## Reproducibility
`python src/generate_data.py --users 10000 --seed 2026` recreates the bundled raw CSVs under the pinned dependencies. `data/raw/manifest.json` records SHA-256 checksums and the observation cutoff. Changing the seed or population changes the findings; rerun validation, SQL, notebooks, and refresh the Tableau data before using new results. Reports and packaged Tableau charts are snapshots of the bundled seed.
